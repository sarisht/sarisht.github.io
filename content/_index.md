I am a postdoctoral researcher at the [University of Michigan](https://cse.engin.umich.edu/), hosted by [Ke Wu](https://kewucse.engin.umich.edu/). I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from [Duke University](https://cs.duke.edu/) in 2026, advised by [Kartik Nayak](https://users.cs.duke.edu/~kartik/) and [Fan Zhang](https://www.fanzhang.me/).

My research studies the security of decentralized systems when the parties running them act strategically. I combine cryptography and algorithmic game theory to design protocols that remain secure when block proposers, builders, and users maximize their own profit. Recent work covers censorship resistance and inclusion lists, transaction fee mechanisms under parallel execution, transaction ordering and MEV, and the trade-off between censorship resistance and throughput in multi-proposer consensus.

I have collaborated with researchers at the Ethereum Foundation, Flashbots, Category Labs, and Common Prefix. If you would like to collaborate, feel free to contact me at [wsarisht@umich.edu](mailto:wsarisht@umich.edu).
